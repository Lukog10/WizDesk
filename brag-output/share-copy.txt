## Long Post (LinkedIn / Community / Product Hunt)
Every developer knows the feeling: you sit down at 9 AM, hit flow state for four hours, and by lunchtime you have completely lost track of what you actually completed and what got left behind.

We built WizDesk to solve that without adding more friction.

WizDesk is a lightweight, privacy-focused desktop companion that lives in your workspace:
1. Autonomous Activity Tracking: Samples your focused window every 5 seconds. No manual start or stop buttons needed.
2. Full Workspace Access: Live stopwatch task timers, subtasks, and instant project distribution donut analytics.
3. 100% Local-First & Zero Telemetry: Your data never leaves your device. Protected by AES-256-GCM encryption and Windows Hello credential gating.
4. Obsidian Vault Bridge: Seamlessly syncs your daily work summaries directly to plaintext Markdown notes in your personal Obsidian vault.

WizDesk v1.2.0 is packaged as a standalone portable Windows executable and is 100% free and open-source on GitHub.

Check out the release: https://github.com/Lukog10/WizDesk
